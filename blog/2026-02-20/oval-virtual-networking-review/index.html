Oval Virtual Networking - A New Era to Network

Published on Feb 20, 2026

Been seeing a lot of "virtual networking" platforms lately, and honestly most of them feel the same. I checked Oval because the concept looked more focused on business events, not just another video call page.

The main idea is simple: bring people into one virtual space, let them talk in real time, and make connecting easier even if everyone is from different places.

What I like is the structure. You get avatar identity, conference-like halls, digital business card exchange, and built-in messaging/video calls. Everything is in one flow, so it feels less scattered.

The speed networking part is interesting too. Avatar mode might sound gimmicky at first, but for online networking it can actually make first conversations less awkward. The chat room setup looks clean and easy to understand.

The exhibitor booth feature is solid. Brands can place banners, videos, brochures, and interactive content in one place. Good for events where sponsors or exhibitors want more than just a logo on a slide.

I haven't tested it deeply yet, so this is just first impression, but the direction is promising. If you're running virtual events and want something more interactive than the usual webinar format, Oval is worth checking. Book at ovalapp.bio/ovalclub/landing.
